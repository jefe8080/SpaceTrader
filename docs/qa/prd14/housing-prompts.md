zusätzlich als Familienreferenz verwendet. Ausgaben mit Alpha-Kanal werden

## Stufe 1

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one isolated game building sprite: level 1 residential low-rise apartment block, three floors, capacity 200 residents (do not write text). Match the reference atlas ONLY for style: isometric fantasy sci-fi Mediterranean comic architecture, warm ivory plaster and terracotta roofs, dark hand-inked contours, teal futuristic utility details, warm upper-left light. Single coherent building, rectangular stone base, viewed from above at 30 degrees, centered entire building with generous transparent margin. No people, no labels, no landscape, no cast shadow outside base. Actual transparent background. This is first of a coherent residential upgrade family; strong readable silhouette at small game zoom.

Generous empty transparent margin.
