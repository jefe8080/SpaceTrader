# PRD 8 – Stadtgrafiken

`agrar-concept.png` ist der am 25.10.2026 vom Nutzer bestätigte erste Stadtentwurf. Er bestimmt Stil und Komposition, wird aber nicht als starres Spielbild verwendet.

## Produktion

Erzeugung mit dem eingebauten `image_gen`-Werkzeug (kein CLI/API-Fallback). Alle konsumierten Grafiken liegen im Projekt unter `client/assets/art/cities/`.

- `sprites.png`: transparente modulare 5×5-Grafiktafel. Zeile 1: Wassertanks, Silos, Kühlhaus, Schüttgut, Maschinen. Zeile 2: Gefahrstoffdepot, Fracht, Passagiere, Technik, Dünger. Zeile 3: Getreidewerk, Gewächshaus, Nahrungsanlage, Rathaus, Schule. Zeile 4: Taverne, Fabrik, Landeplatz, Ruine, Baustelle. Zeile 5: Wohnhaus, Solar, Bettler, Hund, Orbitalring.
- Einzelne Landschaften `agrar.png` bis `ruins.png`: ausreichend große gezeichnete Umgebungen für alle 13 Typen. Wege, Platz, funktionale Gebäude und Wohnbebauung werden dynamisch zusammengesetzt.

Die erste kleinere Umgebungstafel war in der Browserprüfung zu unscharf und wird durch Einzelbilder ersetzt. Die erste Sprite-Erzeugung erhielt eine zweite gezielte Bearbeitung zur Freistellung; die Alpha-Transparenz wurde geprüft. Sprite-Zellen werden beim Laden auf sichtbare Pixel zugeschnitten; getrennte Spiellogik entscheidet über Warenfamilien und Baustände.

## Sprite-Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D game SPRITE ATLAS, one PNG with genuinely TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND. Use attached agricultural city ONLY as style reference: mature European inked painted comic fantasy sci-fi, warm ochre, weathered teal, amber lights, coherent orthographic oblique bird eye projection. Make a precise FIVE COLUMNS by FIVE ROWS equal-cell grid, 25 isolated sprites, one sprite centered fully contained in each cell with transparent margin, NO grid lines NO text NO labels NO background scenery. Consistent view and ground baseline near bottom of every cell, no overlap. Buildings have clear distinct silhouettes and readable details, good at small game size. Row 1 left to right: 1 water depot with twin rounded turquoise tanks and pipes, 2 grain storage tall silver and ochre silos, 3 refrigerated warehouse with frosted white cooling coils, 4 open mineral bulk yard with rock heaps and conveyor, 5 machinery storage open curved hangar with yellow tractor and crane. Row 2: 6 hazard depot armored bunker with sealed acid barrels and warning triangles, 7 general freight depot low ochre rectangular building with colorful crates, 8 passenger terminal with waiting canopy and tiny people, 9 high technology secure depot slim teal glass building antenna and circuit panels, 10 fertilizer barn with sacks pallets and green drums. Row 3: 11 grain production mill with grain elevator, 12 biomass greenhouse with transparent arched roofs and lush plants, 13 food processing bakery with chimney oven and produce crates, 14 civic quest hall with steps arches and teal banners, 15 school with books motif bell and small courtyard. Row 4: 16 tavern with rust orange awnings outdoor tables, 17 manufacturing workshop with copper chimney and open engine bay, 18 landing pad with one small utilitarian science fiction shuttle, 19 ruined shattered ancient human building with vines rubble and broken walls, 20 active construction site with scaffolding and small yellow crane. Row 5: 21 modest rural residential house with small terracotta roof garden, 22 solar panel array with small utility building, 23 seated adult human beggar in worn clothes with bowl beside him ONLY HUMAN FIGURE no architecture, 24 brown dog standing side-on with visible ears legs and tail ONLY ANIMAL no architecture, 25 orbital support station with tall slender pylons and elevated horizontal turquoise metal ring. IMPORTANT humans and dog use their OWN cells and FILL their cells like other sprites so game can scale them down. Every pixel outside the individual subjects MUST be transparent alpha, not dark green, not solid color, not painted checkerboard. No shadows extending into other cells. High detail high resolution clean game-ready atlas.

Gezielte Nachbearbeitung: “Edit this exact 5x5 sprite atlas. REMOVE THE ENTIRE BROWN/OLIVE BACKGROUND and replace with genuine alpha transparency everywhere between and around the 25 separate subjects. Preserve the 25 subjects exactly, their grid locations, their shapes, colors, details and scale. Do not paint a background of any color. Do not add any shadow backdrop. No new objects or layout changes. Output a production transparent sprite sheet suitable for compositing over game terrain. Every space between buildings and between rows is fully transparent. Keep enclosed transparent space around the beggar, dog and ring. Preserve 5 columns 5 rows.”
